### Also worth it (if energy allows)
- **Capitol Grounds** — free to walk, impressive up close. Book guided tour through your rep's office if planned ahead.
- **National Zoo** — free (Smithsonian), pandas are back. A bit north (~2 mi), good for a half-day.

### Practical tips
- August in DC = hot + humid. Plan indoor museums for midday (11am–3pm).
- Wear comfortable shoes. The Mall is ~2 miles end-to-end.
- Sunscreen, water, baby carrier — essential.

## 🥾 AUG 8 · SAT — Shenandoah National Park → Hershey

**⚠️ Early start required. Leave hotel by 7:00–7:30 AM.**

### Drive 1: DC → Shenandoah (~1.5 hrs)
- Take I-66 W → US-340 S to **Front Royal, VA** (north entrance)
- Entry fee: $35/vehicle (valid 7 days). America the Beautiful pass covers it.
- Download offline maps — cell service is spotty inside the park.

### Park Plan (arrive ~9:00 AM, leave by ~3:00 PM)
| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 9:00 AM | Enter at Front Royal | Fill gas before entering — scarce inside |
| 9:15 AM | **Stony Man Trail** (~milepost 41.7) | Best view-for-effort in the park. 1.6 mi round trip, gentle grade. Great for kids. |
| 10:30 AM | **Skyline Drive overlooks** | Pull off at Range View, Hazel Mountain, Pinnacles. Deer everywhere. |
| 12:00 PM | **Big Meadows** (~milepost 51) | Lunch at the lodge or camp store. Large open meadow — prime deer and wildlife viewing. Pick up Junior Ranger booklet for kids. |
| 1:30 PM | **Dark Hollow Falls** (~milepost 50.7) | 1.4 mi round trip to a 70-ft waterfall. Steep on the way back. Most popular hike — start early enough to get parking. |
| 3:00 PM | Exit park, head to Hershey | |

> 💡 **Junior Ranger Program:** Free activity booklet at any visitor center. Kids complete activities and earn an official NPS badge. 10 mins of admin, worth it.

### Drive 2: Shenandoah → Hershey (~2.5 hrs)
- Front Royal → I-66 E briefly → I-81 N → I-78 E → US-322 → Hershey
- Aim to arrive Hershey by **5:30–6:00 PM**

### Evening in Hershey
| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 5:30 PM | Check in: Courtyard Marriott Hershey Chocolate Avenue | 515 E Chocolate Ave — free parking, free cribs, heated indoor pool, Starbucks |
| 6:30 PM | **Hershey's Chocolate World** | Walking distance from hotel. Factory tour ride, chocolate tasting, 4D movie. 2–3 hrs. Open late in summer. |
| 9:00 PM | Back to hotel | Pool for the kids if they have energy |

> 💡 **Chocolate World** is free to enter; individual attractions are ticketed ($5–15 each). The chocolate tour ride is the classic — budget ~$60–80 for the family for a few experiences.

- 🏨 **Hotel:** Courtyard by Marriott Hershey Chocolate Avenue
  - Free parking ✅ | Free cribs ✅ | Heated indoor pool ✅ | Starbucks ✅
  - Budget: ~$130–160/night

## 🎢 AUG 9 · SUN — Hersheypark → Home

### Morning: Hersheypark
| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 8:00 AM | Breakfast at hotel (Starbucks / Bistro) | Fuel up before the park |
| 9:00 AM | **Hersheypark opens** | Buy tickets in advance online — cheaper + skip the gate line |
| 9:00 AM–1:00 PM | Park time | See notes below |
| 1:00–2:00 PM | Lunch, pack up, check out | |

### Hersheypark tips
- **With young kids:** Focus on Founder's Circle (classic rides) and Carrousel Circle. The park has dedicated kiddie areas.
- **Height restrictions:** Most major coasters require 48–54". Check the Hersheypark app for current height charts before visiting.
- **ZooAmerica** is included with park admission — a walkthrough wildlife park adjacent to Hersheypark. Good with a stroller.
- **Buy tickets online** — gate price is ~$80+/person; advance online pricing is significantly cheaper. Check for family bundles.

### Drive Home: Hershey → Pittsburgh (~3.5 hrs)
- Depart by **1:30–2:00 PM** for a comfortable arrival by **5:00–5:30 PM**
- Route: US-322 W → I-78 W → I-76 W (Pennsylvania Turnpike) → Pittsburgh
- Turnpike tolls apply (~$15–20). E-ZPass saves time at plazas.

> 💡 **Good rest stop:** Midway along the PA Turnpike, the travel plazas (Roy Rogers / Starbucks) are clean and baby-friendly.
