Kid Days — Pick 3

Easy local outings from Seven Fields to close out the trip. Mix & match.
🚀

Kamin Science Center

~30 min · North Shore

Hands-on everything: space exhibits, a giant model railroad, and a real WWII submarine (USS Requin) you can tour. Easily a half-day.

📍 Open in Maps
🦁

Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium

~35 min · Highland Park

Zoo + aquarium in one ticket, with robotic dinosaurs around the park for the 2026 season. Go early on a weekday to beat crowds. Hilly — wear good shoes.

📍 Open in Maps
🎢

Kennywood

~40 min · West Mifflin

Historic amusement park — century-old wooden coasters next to modern ones, plus a proper kiddie area. Best as a full day; evenings have drone shows.

📍 Open in Maps
🎨

Children's Museum

~30 min · North Side

Best for younger kids — the water play floor is the star (pack a change of clothes, seriously). Pairs well with the Science Center next door.

📍 Open in Maps
💧

Ohiopyle + Fallingwater

~1.5 hrs · Laurel Highlands

Natural waterslides and easy waterfall walks at Ohiopyle, with Frank Lloyd Wright's Fallingwater nearby (book tour slots ahead — they sell out; closed Wednesdays).

📍 Open in Maps
🚋

Duquesne Incline

~30 min · Mt. Washington

A short century-old cable car ride kids love, ending at the best skyline view in Pittsburgh. Quick outing — great to pair with ice cream or an evening.

Logistics

Know Before You Go

🛂 Border Crossing

  • Passports for everyone, including kids
  • Crossing Aug 1 (into Canada) & Aug 4 (back into the US)
  • Rainbow Bridge at Niagara is the scenic crossing
  • Declare any food/produce in the car

🎟️ Book Ahead

  • Fallingwater tours — timed slots, sell out; closed Wed
  • CN Tower — buy tickets online to skip lines
  • Shenandoah — per-vehicle entrance fee; Old Rag hike needs a separate day-use ticket
  • Kennywood — cheaper online than at the gate

🏖️ Grand Bend Tips

  • Arrive early — parking fills fast on summer Sundays
  • Paid parking (free after 6pm)
  • No tents on the beach; umbrellas OK
  • Stay for sunset — it's the whole point

🚗 Drive-Day Rhythm

  • House rule: wheels rolling by 5–6am on big drive days
  • Longest leg: Brampton → Pittsburgh (~5 hrs)
  • Skyline Drive is 35 mph and winding — it's the destination, not a shortcut
  • Watch for deer at dawn/dusk near Shenandoah
